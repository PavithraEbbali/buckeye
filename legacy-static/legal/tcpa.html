This policy explains how Buckeye Broadband Authorized Agent complies with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and related rules when we call or text you. Your consent and your right to opt out are central to how we operate.

Your consent to be contacted

When you provide your phone number and ask us to contact you — for example by calling us, submitting a request, or clicking to call — you agree that Buckeye Broadband Authorized Agent and the service provider may contact you at that number about your inquiry and related services. This may include calls and text messages, and may use automated or prerecorded technology where you have given the required consent. Message and data rates may apply.

You are never required to agree to marketing calls or texts as a condition of purchasing anything. You can ask us to reach you by email only, and you can withdraw consent at any time.

How and why we contact you

  • To confirm availability at your address and follow up on a request you started.
  • To schedule installation or answer support questions.
  • To share relevant offers — only where you've consented, and always with an easy way to stop.

How to opt out

  • Texts: reply STOP to any message to end texts; reply HELP for help.
  • Calls: tell any representative you wish to be placed on our internal do-not-call list, or email care@buckeyeagent.com.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.

We honor opt-out requests promptly and maintain an internal do-not-call list. We also respect the National Do Not Call Registry for telemarketing.

Records & calling hours

We keep records of consent and opt-out requests. Telemarketing calls, if any, are placed only within the hours permitted by federal and state law. If you believe you received a message in error, contact us at care@buckeyeagent.com and we'll make it right.
