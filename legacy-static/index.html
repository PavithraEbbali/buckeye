01 Are you Buckeye Broadband?

No — we’re an independent, authorized agent. Buckeye Broadband owns the fiber, runs the network and sends the technician. We help you compare the tiers, confirm your street is serviceable and get the order placed.

02 What does fiber actually cost here?

Four residential tiers: $49.99 for 200 Mbps, $59.99 for 400 Mbps, $79.99 for 600 Mbps and $89.99 for a full Gig. Every one carries the 3-Year Price Guarantee, unlimited data and free standard installation. Taxes and fees are extra.

03 What does the 3-Year Price Guarantee really mean?

The number you agree to on the call is the number on your bill for thirty-six months. No promo rate quietly expiring at month thirteen, no annual increase letter — and no long-term contract holding you there.

04 Is my data capped?

Never. Every residential plan is unlimited — no monthly ceiling, no overage line on the bill and no throttling for ordinary household use.

05 Can I add TV, home phone or mobile?

Yes. HD cable TV, home phone and a MaxxMobile 5G line all bolt onto your internet, and qualifying plans include a mobile line at no extra cost. One call, one bill.

06 How fast can someone get here?

Free standard installation is available across most of the footprint, and next-day windows are common. We’ll check your exact address while you’re on the phone and book the earliest slot that suits you.
